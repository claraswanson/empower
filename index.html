Our Mission

As a student-run, student-based organization, EMPOWER aims to improve the quality of life for the people we work with. We are privileged to gain hands-on experience with engineering design projects while empowering our challengers. In addition to design projects, our student organization provides technical resources and education to student members in preparation for post-graduation innovative work.

Projects

Through Empower, Students are albe to participate in 4 different types of projects: Formal Design Projects (FDP), Quarterly Design Projects (QDP), Lab Design Projects (LDP), and Research Projects. To learn more about the different types, check out our project page.
